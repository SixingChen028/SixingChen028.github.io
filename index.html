CV

I am a Ph.D. student in Cognitive Science at New York University, where I am advised by Marcelo Mattar. I am also an AI Safety Research Fellow at the Cambridge Boston Alignment Initiative, mentored by Natalie Shapira.

My research takes two main directions:

  1. I develop theories of how biological neural systems implement computations, and use them to explain flexible behavior and its neural mechanisms. [1] [2] [3]
  2. I use tools from mechanistic interpretability to study reasoning and (mis)alignment in artificial neural systems, particularly language models. [4] [5]

Previously, I earned my B.S. in Cognitive Science from Peking University, where I worked with Hang Zhang on uncertainty representation, and with Dobromir Rahnev at Georgia Tech on metacognition.
